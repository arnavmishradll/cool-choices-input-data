    Under the hood

    Everything the routes are built from. In precomputed mode these are the study area's rasters for the default day; after a live run they switch to that run's corridor.

    1. How a route is computed

    1. Corridor (live): walking paths within of the shortest walking route, plus a shade margin, are modelled.
    2. Weather: hourly air temperature, humidity, wind and sun (direct and diffuse) for the chosen date from Open-Meteo.
    3. SOLWEIG-light: walls, sky view factor, then hour-by-hour shadows, radiation, mean radiant temperature (Tmrt) and UTCI.
    4. Sampling: each path segment gets the mean UTCI, Tmrt and shade of the pixels it crosses, for every hour.
    5. Routing: time-dependent Dijkstra in the browser: each segment is costed at the hour you would reach it.
